Our Story

Global Farm Trust was founded in 2008 by a collective of United Statesn farmers, wildlife carers, and conservation land managers who were underserved by traditional banks. We built a credit union specifically for people whose work sustains our landscapes.

Today we serve over 1,284 members across Colorado, Montana, and Texas — offering farm loans, wildlife grants, and purpose-built financial tools that reward environmental stewardship.

EIN 47-2938104 · FDIC Insured · Member FDIC
🌱
Our Mission
To provide ethical, accessible financial services to the people who protect and nurture United States's wildlife and farmland — while building long-term financial resilience in rural communities.
🦎
Wildlife Commitment
1% of all net revenue is directed to United Statesn wildlife habitat restoration. Our Wildlife Care Fund rewards members directly for planting native trees, building corridors, and supporting biodiversity.

Your privacy matters
Last updated: 1 January 2026 · Effective date: 1 January 2026
1. Information We Collect

Global Farm Trust collects personal information necessary to provide our financial services, including: full name, date of birth, residential and mailing address, email address, phone number, tax file number (TFN), government-issued identity documents, bank account and financial details, and information about your farm or property where relevant to a loan or grant application.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to: verify your identity under the Bank Secrecy Act (BSA); process loan, grant, and account applications; provide ongoing banking services; send account statements and notices; meet our regulatory obligations under the Federal Reserve Act and FDIC prudential standards; and detect and prevent fraud.

3. Disclosure of Information

We may share your information with: credit reporting bodies (Equifax, Experian, illion); government agencies where required by law (ATO, FinCEN, ASIC); our service providers operating under confidentiality agreements; and your authorised representatives. We do not sell your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

4. Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256 encryption. Our systems are audited annually by an independent cybersecurity firm. We maintain ISO 27001-aligned information security management practices and follow FDIC Prudential Standard CPS 234 — Information Security.

5. Your Rights

Under the US Privacy Act and the United Statesn Privacy Principles (APPs), you have the right to: access the personal information we hold about you; correct inaccurate information; complain about a breach of your privacy; and opt out of direct marketing. Contact our Privacy Officer at privacy@globalfarmtrust.com.au.

6. Cookies & Digital Tracking

Our online banking platform uses session cookies for authentication and security. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. Analytics data is anonymised and used solely to improve our services. You can disable cookies in your browser; however this may impact some banking functions.

7. Contact

1. Membership Eligibility

Membership of Global Farm Trust is open to individuals and entities engaged in farming, grazier activities, wildlife care, native plant cultivation, conservation land management, or related rural industries within United States. General membership is also available. Members must be at least 18 years of age and an United Statesn resident or citizen. Global Farm Trust reserves the right to decline or terminate membership in accordance with its Constitution.

2. Account Terms

All accounts are governed by the Product Disclosure Statement (PDS) applicable to each product. Interest rates are variable unless stated as fixed in writing. Global Farm Trust may vary interest rates, fees, or account terms with 20 days' notice via electronic or postal communication. Members are responsible for maintaining secure access credentials and must notify us immediately of any suspected unauthorised access.

3. Loan & Credit Conditions

All loan facilities are subject to credit assessment and approval. Global Farm Trust lends responsibly in accordance with the Truth in Lending Act (TILA) and Dodd-Frank Act (Cth). Loan terms, repayment schedules, and security requirements are set out in individual loan agreements. Early repayment may be subject to break costs for fixed-rate products.

4. Crypto & Digital Asset Deposits

Cryptocurrency deposits are accepted as a funding mechanism only. Global Farm Trust converts received digital assets to USD at the prevailing market rate at time of confirmed receipt. Global Farm Trust is not a digital currency exchange and does not hold member funds in cryptocurrency. Members acknowledge the inherent volatility and irreversibility of blockchain transactions. Global Farm Trust accepts no liability for misdirected or unsupported network deposits.

5. Wildlife Care Fund

Wildlife Credits are awarded at Global Farm Trust's discretion upon verification of eligible activities. Points have no monetary value and cannot be redeemed for cash. Global Farm Trust reserves the right to modify, suspend, or terminate the Wildlife Care Fund program with 30 days' notice. Credits expire if an account is inactive for 24 consecutive months.

6. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, Global Farm Trust's liability for any loss arising from our services is limited to the direct loss caused by our proven negligence. We are not liable for indirect, consequential, or special damages. Nothing in these terms limits any rights you have under the United Statesn Consumer Law.
